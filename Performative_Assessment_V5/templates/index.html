General Guidance

General guidance is shown to the learner before writing. Key points (below) are not — they are used only for scoring. Write this at a higher level of abstraction than the key points; do not name or paraphrase any specific key point.

Key Points

Each key point is a construct (the underlying claim a learner must demonstrate — write it at the level of a claim, not a specific phrasing) plus one or more exemplars (concrete, genuinely different ways to satisfy it — not synonyms of each other). A learner who satisfies the construct through a valid technique not listed here can still be credited during grading as a reviewable "novel equivalent" — see the Review Queue. Not shown to the learner.

Pooled Key Points  — "choose N of M" (optional)

Use a pool when the prompt's own instructions only ask for a limited number of items from a larger set (e.g. "describe at least two techniques"). Each pool has its own importance and a required count — a response earns full credit for the pool once that many members are matched, however many more it also names. Members reuse the same construct + exemplars fields as a standalone key point above, but importance applies to the pool as a whole, not per member.
